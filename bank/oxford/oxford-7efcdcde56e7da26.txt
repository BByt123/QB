-- answer --
Importance of seed and fruit dispersal:
Seed and fruit dispersal can reduce overcrowding and competition for resources (e.g. water, light, nutrients and space) among the daughter and parent plants.	1m
It allows the species to colonize new areas.							1m
The daughter plants can escape from pests and diseases associated with the parent plants.													1m
Methods of seed and fruit dispersal: 
Some seeds are dispersed by explosion. Tension in the fruit wall increases as the pod dries up. This causes the pod to split open and release the seeds inside. /
Some seeds are light and may have 'parachutes'. They can be dispersed by wind. /
Some fruits are buoyant and can be dispersed by water currents. /
Some fruits are brightly coloured and juicy to attract animals to eat them. Their seeds are dispersed when they pass out in the faeces of the animals. /
Some seeds have hooks. They can cling to the hairs of animals. They are carried away from the parent plant and dispersed when the animals move.
