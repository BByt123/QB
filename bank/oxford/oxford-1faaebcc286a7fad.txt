// Schematic diagram // Applying concepts //
(*	The diagram below shows some events involving phagocytes and helper T cells during the invasion of a pathogen.
	
a	Name molecule X.										(1 mark)
b	Describe how the phagocyte at stage II is formed.				(2 marks)
c	With reference to the diagram, briefly explain how non-specific defence mechanisms and specific defence mechanisms work together to fight against the pathogen.									(3 marks)




-- answer --
a	Antigen													1m
b	The pathogen is engulfed and digested by the phagocyte.				1m
Antigens of the pathogen are displayed on the cell membrane of the phagocyte.															1m

(	Describe how vaccination enhances the immunity of an individual.	(9 marks)
 
-- answer --
Vaccines contain live, attenuated pathogens, killed pathogens, inactivated bacterial toxins or viral proteins.											1m
When a vaccine is introduced into the body, the antigen in the vaccine stimulates a primary response.												1m
Memory cells which remember the type of antigen are produced. 			1m
If the body is later invaded by the same antigen, the body will develop a secondary response. 													1m
Memory cells respond quickly by dividing and differentiating into a larger number of plasma cells and killer T cells. 										1m
The secondary response is faster, stronger and lasts longer, and is thus more effective in killing the invading pathogens.									1m
