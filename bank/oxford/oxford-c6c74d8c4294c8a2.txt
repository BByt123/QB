Mitosis is the nuclear division of mitotic cell division. /

During anaphase, the sister chromatids of each chromosome separate and move to the opposite poles of the cell. /
During telophase, new nuclear membranes form around each group of chromosomes. /
The chromosomes uncoil to become chromatin again. They become invisible.
