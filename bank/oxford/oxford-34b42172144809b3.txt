(**A scientist investigated the pattern of inheritance of a genetic disease using DNA fingerprinting. This disease is caused by a single base substitution of a gene. The normal allele of this gene contains a base sequence that can be recognized and cut by a restriction enzyme called EcoRI. The substitution occurred in this sequence.

The scientist collected DNA samples from four members of a family and produced copies of the DNA segments containing the gene using PCR. He then cut the DNA segments with EcoRI and separated the fragments produced by gel electrophoresis. The diagram below shows the pedigree of this family and the DNA fingerprints of the four members.
	
a	Apart from the positions of the bands, describe one difference in the patterns of bands in the DNA fingerprints of individuals 2 and 4. Suggest a reason for it.											(3 marks)
b	Based on the DNA fingerprints and the pedigree, deduce whether the mutated allele is dominant or recessive. Briefly explain your answer.																(4 marks)
c	Based on the DNA fingerprints and the pedigree, suggest whether the gene is X-linked or not. Briefly explain your answer.				(2 marks)


-- answer --
a	There were two bands in the DNA fingerprint of individual 2 and only one band in that of individual 4.											1m
Individual 2 possesses two normal alleles. The DNA segments containing the normal allele were cut by the restriction enzyme. Therefore, two bands were produced.													1m
Individual 4 possesses two mutated alleles. The DNA segments containing the mutated allele were not cut by the restriction enzyme as base substitution occurred in the recognition site. Therefore, only one band was produced.			1m
b	The DNA fingerprints of individuals 1 and 3 both contained 3 bands, two from the normal allele and one from the mutated allele.						1m
Therefore, individuals 1 and 3 are heterozygous.						1m
From the pedigree, we can see that both individuals 1 and 3 are normal.	1m
Since only the dominant allele can be expressed in the heterozygous condition, the mutated allele should be recessive.							1m
c	The gene is not X-linked.										1m
Because individual 3 is a male and he possesses two alleles of the gene.	1m
Note:
If the gene is X-linked, males will possess only one allele of the gene because they have only one X chromosome.
