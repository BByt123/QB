*	Explain Mendel's first law and second law of inheritance with events occurring in meiotic cell division.										(5 marks)

-- answer --
Mendel's first law of inheritance:
Alleles of each gene are located on a pair of homologous chromosomes and the 	two members of the homologous pair separate during anaphase I.				1m
Each gamete receives one member of the homologous pair and hence its allele.																	1m
Mendel's second law of inheritance:
Homologous pairs line up randomly along the equator of the cell during metaphase I.																1m
The members of each homologous pair then separate independently of the 	other pairs of chromosomes during anaphase I.									1m
The separation of alleles for one gene on a pair of homologous chromosomes is therefore independent of the separation of alleles for other genes located on the other chromosomes.													1m
