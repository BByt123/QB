*	Describe the actions of enzymes. Name two enzymes in our body and describe their roles. 												(10 marks)
-- answer --
Actions of enzymes: 
Enzymes are biological catalysts. /
There is an active site of a specific shape on each enzyme molecule. /
An enzyme molecule binds to substrate molecules that fit the shape of its active site. /
The formation of the enzyme-substrate complex greatly lowers the activation energy of the reaction. /
The substrate(s) are converted into product(s), which dissociate from the enzyme molecule. 	/
The enzyme molecule is released in its original form. (any 5)			1m x 5
Examples:
Salivary amylase in saliva / pancreatic amylase in pancreatic juice catalyses the breakdown of starch into maltose. /
Carbohydrases on the cell membranes of the epithelial cells in the small intestine catalyse the breakdown of disaccharides into monosaccharides. / 
Pepsin in gastric juice catalyses the breakdown of proteins into peptides. / Proteases in pancreatic juice / on the cell membranes of the epithelial cells in the small intestine catalyse the breakdown of proteins into peptides / peptides into amino acids. /
Pancreatic lipase in the pancreatic juice catalyses the breakdown of lipids into fatty acids and glycerol. /
DNA polymerase catalyses the addition of free nucleotides to a new DNA strand during DNA replication. (any 2 or other correct answers) 					1m x 2
