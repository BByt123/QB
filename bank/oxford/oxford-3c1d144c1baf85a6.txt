*	Describe the major steps of DNA fingerprinting and discuss the application of DNA fingerprinting in forensic science.							(12 marks)

-- answer --
DNA fingerprinting is a technique to identify an individual based on a DNA sample.																1m
It makes use of the difference in the base sequence on the DNA between individuals.																1m
To produce a DNA fingerprint of an individual, DNA is first extracted from the cells of the individual. 													1m
DNA fragments containing selected regions (e.g. highly variable regions) are obtained.																1m
The DNA fragments are separated according to their size using gel electrophoresis.																1m
The pattern of bands obtained after gel electrophoresis is the DNA fingerprint. It is unique to the individual. 											1m
DNA fingerprints can provide evidence on the identities of individuals in court cases. 																1m
The DNA fingerprint produced using the DNA sample collected at the crime scene can be used to compare with that of the suspect. 							1m
If the band patterns match, the sample collected at the crime scene is likely to have come from the suspect. 											1m
