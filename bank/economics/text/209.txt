Question code: B2C09Q053
When the percentage contribution of secondary production to the total value of production increases,
A.	the percentage contribution of primary production to the total value of production must decrease.
B.	the percentage contribution of tertiary production to the total value of production must decrease.
C.	the total percentage contribution of primary and tertiary production to the total value of production must decrease.
D.	All of the above

Answer: C
Explanation
•	The total percentage contribution of the three types of production to the total value of production must add up to 100%. Therefore, when the percentage contribution of secondary production to the total value of production increases, the total percentage contribution of primary and tertiary production must decrease. 
Option C is the answer.
•	However, we cannot conclude that the percentage contribution of primary production or the percentage contribution of tertiary production must decrease. Options A and B are incorrect.
