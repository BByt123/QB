Question code: B3C16Q012
* Which of the following imply that resource allocation is efficient?
(1)	The total social surplus is maximised.
(2)	Producers produce up to a point where MB = P = MC.
(3)	Both consumers and producers try to maximise their own interests.
(4)	The consumer surplus is maximised.
A.	(1) and (2) only
B.	(1) and (3) only
C.	(2) and (3) only
D.	(3) and (4) only

Answer: A
Explanation
Option (3) is not the answer. It is the basic assumption of the behaviour of consumers and producers. Even if both consumers and producers try to maximise their own interests, this does not guarantee that the market is efficient. For example, in a monopoly, both consumers and producers will also maximise their gains. However, the market is still inefficient.

Question code: B3C16Q049
* Refer to the diagram below.

When the output level is at Qf, there is a deadweight loss because
A.	the market price is at Px, which is lower than the equilibrium price.
B.	Pf > Px, which implies that there is a potential loss from an increase in the output level.
C.	Pf > Px, which implies that total social surplus can be increased by producing more.
D.	the market price is at Pf, which implies that producers do not maximise their producer surplus.
Answer: C
Explanation
Pf is the price after the imposition of a price floor, which is also equal to the marginal benefit at Qf. Px is the marginal cost at Qf. Thus, Pf > Px implies that the marginal benefit is greater than the marginal cost at Qf. In other words, the marginal social surplus (= MB - MC) is greater than zero. There is a potential gain from an increase in the output level.
