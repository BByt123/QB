Question code: B2C11Q015
* Which of the following statements about the relationship between marginal product, average product and total product is correct? 
A.	When marginal product increases, total product increases. 
B.	When marginal product decreases, total product decreases. 
C.	Average product is total product divided by the quantity of fixed factors. 
D.	None of the above 



Answer: D
Explanation
For Option A, suppose the marginal product of the nth unit of a variable factor (e.g., labour) is -5 units of output, while the total product using n units of labour is 100 units of output. If the marginal product of the (n＋1) th unit of labour is -3, the marginal product increases (from -5 to -3), while the total product using (n＋1) units of labour decreases to 97 (100 - 3) units of output. Therefore, Option A is incorrect. 
For Option B, suppose the marginal product of the nth unit of labour is 10 units of output, while the total product using n units of labour is 100 units of output. If the marginal product of the (n＋1) th unit of labour is 5 units of output, the marginal product decreases (from 10 to 5) and the total product using (n＋1) units of labour increases from 100 units of output to 105 units of output. Therefore, Option B is incorrect.
