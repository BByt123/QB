Short Questions

Question code: B7C15Q301 (new)
(a)	What is economic growth?	(2 marks)
(b)	Explain why it is better to measure economic growth by per capita real GDP than real GDP.	(2 marks)

Answers:
(a)	Economic growth refers to an increase in the real value of an economy's production.	(2 marks)
(b)	An increase in an economy's real GDP may be due to an increase in population. Per capita real GDP eliminates the effect of changes in population.	(2 marks)

Question code: B7C15Q302 (new)
Briefly explain why the Human Development Index is a better measurement of economic development than per capita real GDP.	(3 marks)

Answers:
Per capita real GDP can be used to measure economic growth. However, economic development takes into account aspects other than economic growth. Besides economic growth, the HDI also takes into account an economy's level of health and educational attainment.	(3 marks)

Question code: B7C15Q303
List the three measures of the Human Development Index (HDI) and briefly explain how they reflect a country's standard of living.	(6 marks)

Answers:
HDI is based on the following three measures:
(	Life expectancy at birth - If an economy has better healthcare, its people can enjoy a longer, healthier life. As a result, the country's productivity and living standards will be higher.	(2 marks)
(	Years of schooling - This consists of two indicators, mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling. More and better education can increase people's living standards.	(2 marks)

(	Per capita GNI (PPP) - Countries with higher per capita GNI (PPP) generally have higher living standards.	(2 marks)

Question code: B7C15Q309
Student A says, 'Since a higher level of saving will reduce consumption and hence aggregate demand, a higher level of saving will harm economic growth.' Do you agree? Explain.	(3 marks)

Answers:
No. If the savings are used for investment purposes, the increase in investment will compensate for the decrease in consumption. As a result, the economy's real income may not fall, while the increase in investment will help increase the economy's future income.	(3 marks)
