Question code: B6C07Q029
* Which of the following statements about money is correct?
A.	Without money, there will be no indirect exchange.
B.	Without money, there will be no problem of inflation.
C.	Without money, there will be no future payments.
D.	The types of money must be dictated by the government.

Answer: B
Explanation
Option B is correct. If there is no money, the value of goods is expressed in terms of other goods. Thus, the prices of all goods will only be measured in real value, instead of nominal (monetary) value. The problem of inflation will not exist as there is no monetary value.

Question code: B6C07Q030
* Which of the following statements about money is correct?
A.	Money raises the cost of exchange because people have to make change.
B.	Inflation occurs when the money supply keeps increasing.
C.	Sufficient money supply is a precondition for exchange.
D.	Only the central bank can issue money.

Answer: B
Explanation
Option B is correct. When the money supply increases, the value of money falls, and each unit of money can buy fewer goods. That is, the purchasing power of money will decrease and the price of goods in terms of money will increase. Thus, when the money supply keeps increasing, the general price level will keep increasing and inflation occurs.

	(2 marks)

(c)	In Year 3, Ming lost a lot of money in the stock market. He decided to close his factory and use garments to pay his workers all wages in arrears (欠薪). Some workers accepted the arrangement.
(i)	List and define TWO functions of the garments similar to those of money in this case.	(4 marks)
(ii)	Explain why garments are NOT money.	(3 marks)

Answers:
(a)	(i)	(	The business cycle refers to short run fluctuations in real GDP around the long run trend.	(2 marks)
(	The four phases are contraction, trough, expansion and peak.
(0.5 marks for each phase)
(ii)	Contraction as the real GDP is declining.	(2 marks)

(b)	(i)	Time rate (hour rate). The workers have an incentive to work longer and produce fewer goods.	(2 marks)
(ii)	Piece rate.	(1 mark)
Advantages:
(	It could raise workers' incentive to work and their productivity.
(	It could reduce the need for supervision against shirking.
(Accept other reasonable answers, 1 mark × 2 = 2 marks)

(c)	(i)	(	As a medium of exchange: Something accepted for the exchange of goods and services
(	As a store of value: Something used to preserve purchasing power and accumulate wealth

(ii)	Money is anything that is generally accepted as a medium of exchange.
	(2 marks)
Since garments are not generally accepted, they are not money.	(1 mark)

Question code: B6C07Q505
In a remote area, people live self-sufficiently. Recently, they have decided to specialise in producing goods that can be exchanged for dried fruit. Then, they will use their dried fruit to exchange for other goods they want.
(a)	Explain whether people who are self-sufficient need money.	(2 marks)
(b)	Suppose dried fruit is accepted in all transactions. Explain whether dried fruit is money in the above case.	(2 marks)
(c)	Explain the functions of money served by dried fruit in the cases below.
	(i)	A hawker said, 'My chickens are fresh, each costing 50 pieces of dried fruit.'	(2 marks)
	(ii)	Mary stored a lot of dried fruit in her warehouse for future purchase.	(2 marks)
(d)	State THREE desirable properties of money which dried fruit does not have.
	(6 marks)

Answers:
(a)	They do not need money because there is no exchange.	(2 marks)

(b)	Yes	(1 mark)
	because dried fruit is a generally accepted medium of exchange.	(1 mark)

(c)	(i)	Unit of account,	(1 mark)
		because the price of another good (chickens) is expressed in terms of dried fruit.	(1 mark)
	(ii)	Store of value,	(1 mark)
		because dried fruit is used to preserve purchasing power and accumulate wealth.	(1 mark)

(d)	Dried fruit does not have the following desirable properties of money:
(	Durable: Dried fruit is perishable.
(	Portable: Dried fruit is not portable, especially in large transactions.
(	Homogeneous: Dried fruit is not homogeneous because it does not possess uniform physical characteristics.
(	Stable in supply: The supply of dried fruit is unstable and its value may fluctuate greatly.
(Any THREE of the above, 2 marks × 3 = 6 marks)
