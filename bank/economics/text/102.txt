Question code: B1C02Q045
Which of the following is a feature of a market-oriented economy?
A.	The problem of scarcity is more serious.
B.	There is price competition only.
C.	Resources are allocated according to the will of government officials. 
D.	Most productive resources are privately owned.

Answer: D
Explanation
•		Scarcity means that our resources are insufficient to satisfy all our wants. In all types of economies, scarcity exists. The difference between different types of economies lies in their different methods to solve the three basic economic problems.
•	In a market-oriented economy, there are some resources which are not allocated by price mechanism. Other forms of competition replace price competition to decide who gets these resources.
