Question code: B3C15Q028
The following table shows the production cost of a price-taking firm.

Total output (units)	1	2	3	4	5	
Marginal cost ($)	2	4	6	8	10	
Average variable cost ($)	2	3	4	5	6	
Fixed cost ($)	2	2	2	2	2	

If the product price is $8,
A.	the firm will produce 5 units of output to maximise its profit.
B.	the maximum profit the firm can make is $10.
C.	the average cost is $5 when the firm maximises its profit.
D.	the producer surplus is $10.

Answer: B
Explanation:
If the product price is $8, the profit maximising output is 4 units where the marginal cost is equal to the price. At this output, the producer surplus is $12 [= ($8 × 4) - ($5 × 4)], while the profit is $10 [= ($8 × 4) - ($5 × 4) - $2]. The average cost is $5.5 
[= $22 / 4].

Question code: B3C15Q069
Refer to the following supply and demand schedules for toys.

0	6	0	
2	5	1
