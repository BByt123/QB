Question code: B6C08Q013
* Which of the following statements about the monetary base is INCORRECT?
A.	The monetary base will not change during the money creation process.
B.	An increase in the monetary base will increase the maximum possible amount of money supply in the economy.
C.	Cash leakage will not affect the value of the monetary base.
D.	When there is no cash leakage, the monetary base is the same as reserves held by the banking system.

Answer: D
Explanation
Option A is a correct statement. During the money creation process, only credit and deposits are created, and the total amount of legal tender issued in the economy (i.e., the monetary base) remains unchanged.
Option B is a correct statement. When members of the public deposit cash into the banking system, the money supply will increase after the money creation process. Hence, the monetary base determines the maximum amount of cash that can be used for money creation, and also determines the maximum possible amount of money supply in the economy. When the monetary base increases, the maximum possible amount of money supply will increase.
Option D is the answer. No cash leakage means all the loans from banks are redeposited into the banking system. This does not mean that the public does not hold cash.

Question code: B6C08Q050
* Which of the following situations will NEVER happen?
A.	Excess reserves are larger than required reserves.
B.	The legal reserve ratio is smaller than the actual reserve ratio.
C.	After the deposit creation process, the maximum increase in deposits is equal to the maximum increase in money supply.
D.	After the deposit creation process, the maximum increase in reserves is greater than the initial deposit.

Answer: D
Explanation
Option D is the answer. When a certain amount of cash (say, $1,000) is deposited into a bank, the bank can lend out excess reserves (say, $800). If the loan is redeposited into the banking system (i.e., no cash leakage), the total increase in reserves in the banking system (= $200 + $800 = $1,000) is equal to the initial deposit. Hence, during the deposit creation process, only the amounts of loans and deposits increase, not reserves. The maximum increase in reserves is equal to the initial deposit. (Remark: If there is cash leakage, the actual increase in reserves will be smaller than the maximum increase in reserves, i.e., the initial deposit.)
