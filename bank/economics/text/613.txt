Question code: B6C13Q303 (new)
From time to time, the US urges the Chinese government to raise the value of the renminbi. Explain how the rise in the value of renminbi affects
(a)	prices of Chinese goods in terms of US dollars (given their prices in renminbi remain unchanged);	(2 marks)
(b)	the export volume of US goods to China.	(2 marks)

Answers:
(a)	After the rise in the value of the renminbi (i.e., appreciation of the renminbi), more US dollars are needed in exchange for each unit of renminbi. Hence, when the renminbi prices of Chinese goods remain unchanged, the prices in terms of US dollars will increase.	(2 marks)
(b)	After the rise in the value of the renminbi, the prices of US goods in terms of the renminbi decrease. The quantity demanded of US goods in China increases. 
Hence, the export volume of US goods to China will increase.	(2 marks)
