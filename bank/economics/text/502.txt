Which of the following descriptions about GDP is correct?

B.	Measuring GDP at base-year prices can eliminate the effect of price changes on GDP.

D.	Per capita nominal GDP must be higher than per capita real GDP.

Answer: B
Explanation
Option C is not correct. Percentage change in real GDP ~ Percentage change in nominal GDP - Percentage change in price level. If the percentage decrease in nominal GDP is smaller than the percentage decrease in the price level, the percentage change in real GDP will be positive, i.e., real GDP will rise.

Question code: B5C02Q304

	(4 marks)

Answers:
A change in nominal GDP in different periods can be caused by a change in quantities and/or a change in prices.	(1 mark)

(c)	Suppose the total populations on Monkey Island in Year 1 and Year 2 are 1 million and 1.4 million, respectively.
(i)	Show the relationship between per capita nominal GDP and nominal GDP by an equation.	(1 mark)
(ii)	Calculate the per capita GDP at market prices for each year.	(2 marks)
(iii)	Explain why per capita nominal GDP is a better indicator of economic welfare, compared with nominal GDP.	(1 mark)

(i)	What would be the most probable change in the economic welfare of Monkey Island in Year 2?	(3 marks)

Answers:
(a)	GDP is the total value of production of all resident producing units of an economy in a specified period.	(2 marks)

(b)	GDP at market prices in Year 1 
	= $[100 + (30 + 20) + 50 + 10] million	(1 mark)
= $210 million	(1 mark)
GDP at market prices in Year 2 
= $[10 + (30 + 20) + 120 + 100] million	(1 mark)
= $280 million	(1 mark)

(c)	(i)	Per capita nominal GDP = Nominal GDP ÷ Population	(1 mark)
(ii)	Per capita GDP at market prices in Year 1 = $(210 ÷ 1) = $210	(1 mark)
Per capita GDP at market prices in Year 2 = $(280 ÷ 1.4) = $200	(1 mark)
(iii)	Per capita nominal GDP eliminates the effect of population size while nominal GDP does not.	(1 mark)

(d)	(i)	As there is no change in the general price level, the increase in nominal GDP implies that there is an increase in real GDP in Year 2.	(1 mark)
As there is no change in population, the increase in real GDP implies that there is an increase in per capita real GDP in Year 2.	(1 mark)
Thus, economic welfare in Year 2 will probably increase.	(1 mark)
(ii)	An increase in private consumption expenditure can improve current economic welfare. Economic welfare in Year 2 may be lower as there is a decrease in private consumption expenditure.	(1 mark)
The increase in nominal GDP is due to the increase in net exports. The exported goods are consumed by non-local households. Thus, economic welfare in Year 2 may be overestimated.	(1 mark)

(e)	(i)	GDP is unaffected as the bananas are not produced in Year 2.	(2 marks)
As there is an increase in private consumption expenditure, economic welfare in Year 2 will improve.	(2 marks)
(ii)	GDP is unaffected as the value of volunteer services is not counted in the GDP.	(2 marks)
There is an improvement in economic welfare as volunteer work improves economic welfare.	(2 marks)
