Question code: B2C10Q022
* Study the following production information about Country A and Country B.

	Country A	Country B	
 Labour force	3 million	2 million	
 Output (units)	600	400	

Which of the following descriptions must be correct?
	(1)	The labour supply of Country A is higher than that of Country B.
	(2)	The quality of labour in Country A is higher than that of Country B.
	(3)	The average labour productivity of Country A is the same as that of Country B.
A.	(1) only
B.	(1) and (2) only
C.	(1) and (3) only
D.	None of the above

Answer: D

Explanation
•	While labour supply is positively related to the size of the labour force and the average working hours of workers, the latter may be much higher in Country B.
•	The labour force usually includes both employed and unemployed persons. On the other hand, (average) labour productivity is measured by the output produced per working hour of labour. As we do not have information on the latter, we cannot conclude which country has a higher average labour productivity.
