Question code: B1C06Q057
* An economic researcher comes up with the following data about Good X:

Price ($)	10	12	14	16	18	
Quantity transacted (units)	100	200	300	250	200	

If the demand curve for Good X is downward-sloping and the supply curve for it is upward-sloping, what is the equilibrium price?
A.	$10
B.	$12
C.	$14
D.	$16

Answer: C
Explanation:
At the equilibrium price (Pe), Qd = Qs = Qt, given that demand curve is downward sloping and supply curve is upward sloping. When the market price is above the equilibrium price, Qd falls while Qs rises; Qt equals Qd because whenever Qd is not equal to Qs, Qt is equal to the smaller of the two quantities. Similarly, when the market price is smaller than the equilibrium price, Qd rises while Qs falls; Qt equals Qs. Hence, Qt is the largest at the equilibrium price. Therefore, the equilibrium price from the above table is the one with the largest Qt, i.e., $14.

Question code: B1C06Q502
Suppose the government of Thailand announces a rice support programme. Under the programme, the government will directly purchase rice from local farmers for a price that is 50% above the market level. However, in recent years, Thailand has faced fierce competition from Vietnam and India in the market for exported rice.
*(a)	How will the above programme affect rice farmers' total income in Thailand? Explain with the aid of a diagram.	(6 marks)
(b)	How will the above programme affect the ratio of Thai rice to Vietnamese rice consumed by Hong Kong people? Explain.	(6 marks)

(	Both price and quantity transacted increase.	(1 mark)
(	Total income increases.	(1 mark)
	
	Verbal elaboration:
