Question code: B1C01Q001
Economics is the study of
A.	how to make rational investments.
B.	how to solve the problem of scarcity.
C.	how to allocate resources under scarcity.

Question code: B1C01Q039 (new)
* Oliver has bought air tickets between Hong Kong and London for $13,000. He plans to see a football match there and has bought a ticket for a match between Arsenal and Liverpool for $2,000. Recently, a company offered Oliver a summer job opportunity from which he may earn $12,000.If he accepts this offer, he will not have time to travel to London. Oliver finds that he can sell the ticket for the match online for $2,500. However, the air tickets are not refundable. What is Oliver's cost of seeing the match?
A.	$13,500
B.	$14,500
C.	$15,000
D.	$27,500

Answer: B
Explanation
•	Full cost = Cost of using the match ticket + Time cost
•	The cost of using the match ticket is $2,500, instead of $2,000. The $2,000 is the cost that Oliver paid which he cannot recover. Only the resell price of $2,500 is an option available to Oliver at present.
•	The time cost is $12,000, which is the value of the best alternative use of the time.
•	Therefore, the full cost = $2,500 + $12,000 = $14,500.
•	The price of the air tickets should not be considered as it has been paid and the tickets are not refundable.

Question code: B1C01Q044
Mary is deciding which of two ways to spend her weekend:
	(I)	Watching a film, for which she has already bought a ticket
	(II)	Tutoring a primary student to earn $200
Which of the following will increase Mary's opportunity cost of watching the film?

Question code: B1C01Q054
Today is Fiona's birthday. She can have dinner with her boyfriend tonight. If she does not have dinner with him, she can work as a model for an advertisement for $20,000. She can also perform in a show for $15,000. What is Fiona's cost of choosing to have dinner with her boyfriend tonight?
A.	$35,000
B.	$20,000
C.	$15,000
D.	$0

Answer: B
Explanation
•	Cost is the highest-valued option forgone in making a choice. If Fiona does not choose to have dinner with her boyfriend, she has two options: earning $20,000 as a model for an advertisement and earning $15,000 as a reward for her performance in a show. Among the two options, earning $20,000 as a model in an advertisement (Option B) is the highest-valued option forgone.
•	Since Fiona cannot appear in the advertisement and the show at the same time, her cost of choosing to have dinner with her boyfriend is not the sum of the incomes from these two jobs, i.e. $15,000 + $20,000 = $35,000 (Option A).

(b)	•	In this case, when James chooses to pay the workers' wages, the opportunity cost is purchasing advanced equipment.	(1 mark)
