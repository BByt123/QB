Question code: B1C07Q006 (new)
Refer to the following supply-demand diagram of a good. The equilibrium price is $26.

A.	1:2
B.	2:3
C.	1:1
D.	2:1

Answer: D
Explanation:
After the imposition of the $12 unit tax, the supply curve shifts upward by $12. The market price will increase from $26 to $34. Hence, the amount of unit tax borne by consumers is $8. The amount of unit tax borne by producers is $4. As a result the ratio of the consumers' tax burden to the producers' tax burden = $8 : $4 = 2:1.
