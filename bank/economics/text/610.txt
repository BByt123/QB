Question code: B6C10Q025
* Which of the following are possible effects of raising the discount rate?
(1)	The cost of holding money increases.
(2)	Private banks' cost of borrowing from the central bank increases.
(3)	The real return on money decreases.
A.	(1) and (2) only
B.	(1) and (3) only
C.	(2) and (3) only
D.	(1), (2) and (3)

Answer: A
Explanation
The discount rate is the interest rate that the central bank charges when it lends money to private banks. Option (2) is correct.
When there is an increase in the discount rate, money supply will decrease and so the nominal interest rate (i.e., the cost of holding money) will increase. Hence, Option (1) is correct.
The decrease in aggregate demand will lead to a decrease in the price level. As the real return on money (in percentage) = - inflation rate, the real return on money will increase. Therefore, Option (3) is incorrect.

Question code: B6C10Q027
* When the central bank lowers the discount rate, the money supply curve will
A.	shift rightwards because the cost of holding money decreases.
B.	shift rightwards only if there is no cash leakage.
C.	shift leftwards because banks will borrow less from other banks.
D.	remain unchanged if people do not borrow money from banks.

Answer: D
Explanation
Option B is incorrect. The assumption of 'no cash leakage' is needed only in the case of maximum creation of money. Even if there is cash leakage, money creation may still take place if some loans are redeposited into the banking system.
Option D is correct. If people do not borrow money from banks, banks cannot create new loans. Thus, the money supply curve will not change.
