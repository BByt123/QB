Answers: 
(a)	•	Market share maximisation
	•	Corporate social responsibility
	•	Provision of non-profitmaking goods and services
	(Any TWO of the above. 1 mark × 2 = 2 marks)
(b)	In a perfectly competitive market, the marginal revenue is equal to the market price of a good, which is constant.	(1 mark)
	Assume that the marginal cost of a firm is increasing. When the marginal revenue is equal to the marginal cost, the marginal cost of producing an additional unit will be greater than the marginal revenue. In other words, the firm cannot increase its profit by increasing output. Therefore, profit is maximised when the marginal revenue is equal to the marginal cost.	(4 marks)

Question code: B2C14Q310
Explain why in a price-taking market, the supply schedule of a firm coincides with its marginal cost schedule.	(4 marks)

Answers:
•	Facing an upwardly sloping marginal cost curve, the firm can maximise its profits by producing at the quantity at which marginal cost, marginal revenue and price are equal to each other. Then, all the units with MC ( MR are produced.	(2 marks)
•	The profit-maximising outputs at different prices are actually the quantities on the marginal cost schedules whose marginal costs are equal to the prices. Therefore, the supply schedule of the firm coincides with its marginal cost schedule.		(2 marks)
