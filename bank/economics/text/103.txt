Question code: B1C03Q009
Which of the following persons has demand for a Gucci handbag priced at $15,000?
A.	A bankrupt (破產者) who wants the handbag very much
B.	An economics professor with a monthly income over $60,000 who hates branded products
C.	The wife of a rich businessman who is fond of the bag and can afford it
D.	All of the above


Answer: C
Explanation
•	The bankrupt wants the Gucci handbag very much but cannot spend money on luxuries. Therefore, he or she does not have a demand for it.
•	Although the professor has the money to buy the handbag, he or she is not willing to buy it. Therefore, he or she has no demand for it. 
•	Only when the person has the ability and willingness to buy the handbag will the demand for it exist. Therefore, only the wife of a rich businessman has the demand for it.
