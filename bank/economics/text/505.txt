Question code: B5C05Q018
* Which of the following statements are INCORRECT?
(1)	When the government lowers the unit tax of a good and given the same aggregate output demanded, the price level drops. Thus, the aggregate demand curve shifts downwards.
(2)	When people desire to invest more, they will consume less. As a result, private consumption expenditure falls and the aggregate demand curve shifts leftwards.
(3)	When people's wealth increases and given the same price level, individuals have a greater ability to consume. Thus, private consumption expenditure rises and the aggregate demand curve shifts rightwards.
A.	(1) and (2) only
B.	(1) and (3) only
C.	(2) and (3) only
D.	All of the above

Answer: A
Explanation
Option (1) is the answer. The decrease in unit tax will shift the short run aggregate supply curve (instead of aggregate demand curve) downwards. Moreover, the decrease in the unit tax of a particular good may only have a small (or even negligible) effect on the short run aggregate supply.
Option (2) is the answer. When people desire to invest more, gross investment expenditure will increase. Even if they cut their consumption and save more money to finance their investment, aggregate demand may not decrease. Thus, the aggregate demand curve may not shift leftwards.

Question code: B5C05Q063
Country A and Country B are trading partners. If the price level in Country B increases, Country A's real GDP will ________ in the short run.
A.	increase
B.	remain unchanged
C.	decrease
D.	be indeterminate

Answer: A
Explanation
When Country B's price level increases, the products that Country B makes will become more expensive. Country B's consumers will buy a larger quantity of Country A's goods and Country A will import a smaller quantity of Country B's goods. The real value of Country A's net exports rises and Country A's aggregate demand curve will shift to the right. As a result, Country A's real GDP will increase in the short run.
