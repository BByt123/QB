Question code: B5C03Q029
Real value in a specified period
A.	is value expressed in units of money in the base period.

Question code: B5C03Q040
* Study the following diagram about the GDP deflator in Forbidden Island.

A.	In Year 1, providers of fixed future payments gain.
B.	In Year 1, there was no inflationary effect.

D.	From Year 4 to Year 5, recipients of fixed future payments lose.

Explanation
To answer this question, students should first find the differences between the actual inflation rate and the expected inflation rate.
