Question code: B6C12Q021 (new)
* Country C was a small, closed economy. Recently, the government decided to allow international trade in Good X. However, to protect its local industries, it has imposed a tariff on Good X. Which of the following are the overall effects of these changes?
(1)	The domestic price of Good X will increase.
(2)	Domestic producers of Good X will gain.
(3)	Domestic consumers of Good X will lose.
A.	(1) and (2) only
B.	(2) and (3) only
C.	(1), (2) and (3)
D.	None of the above

Answer: D
Explanation
The tariff only reduces the effects of opening the economy. Thus, compared with a situation when there is no trade, after opening the economy and imposing a tariff, both the domestic price and domestic production will decrease, while domestic consumption will increase. As a result, domestic producers will lose while domestic consumers will gain.

Question code: B6C12Q022 (new)
* Suppose Country A exports Good X to Country B. If Country B imposes a tariff on Good X, how will this affect Country A?
A.	Country A's consumers will lose.
B.	Country A's producers will gain.
C.	In Country A, the quantity transacted of Good X will decrease.
D.	The price of Good X in Country A may decrease.

Answer: D
Explanation
Option D is correct. When Country B imposes a tariff on Good X, the quantity of Country A's exports will decrease. Some resources may be reallocated to Country A's domestic production. Thus, in Country A, the supply of Good X may increase and so the price of Good X may decrease.

Question code: B6C12Q312
Briefly explain how the imposition of quotas affects the quality of imports.	(3 marks)

Answers:
Importers have to pay a cost to compete for quotas. After the addition of a unit quota-cost, the money prices of higher quality imports rise by a smaller percentage than the money prices of lower quality imports. In other words, the relative prices of higher quality imports drop. By the law of demand, the importing country will import a greater proportion of higher quality goods. Hence, the average quality of imports improves.	(3 marks)
