Question code: B3C18Q001
Which of the following about equity is NOT correct?
A.	Even if a market economy achieves efficiency in resource allocation, it may fail to achieve equity.
B.	Different people may have different standards of equity.
C.	Equity is a subjective concept.
D.	Equity is the same as equal distribution of income.

Answer: D

Question code: B3C18Q002
Which of the following countries has achieved equity in income distribution?
A.	Country A, where all workers receive the same wage
B.	Country B, where all outputs are owned by the state and distributed equally to all people
C.	Country C, where hardworking people earn more income
D.	Insufficient information to make a determination

Answer: D
Explanation
Equity is a subjective concept that involves normative judgements. Different people can have different criteria about fairness.

	(2 marks)
(c)	Is the education provided by the government a free good?	(3 marks)
(d)	Is knowledge a public good?	(4 marks)

Answers:
(a)	(	Efficiency is attained when total social surplus is maximised.	(1 mark)

	(2 marks)

(b)	(	Education can provide equal opportunities for the poor to improve their living standards. This helps equalise income distribution.	(2 marks)

(c)	(	Free goods are goods whose available quantity is sufficient to satisfy all human wants for them. No one is willing to pay a price for them.	(1 mark)

(d)	(	Public goods are goods which are non-rival and non-excludable in consumption.	(1 mark)
(	Non-rivalry in consumption means that one's consumption does not reduce the amount available for other's consumption.	(1 mark)
(	Non-excludability in consumption means that it is too difficult to prevent non-payers from consuming the good.	(1 mark)
(	It is difficult to prevent non-payers from using knowledge, and everyone can use that knowledge at the same time. Therefore, knowledge is a public good.
